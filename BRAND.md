# Myco: Evidence Trace

Context becomes a trace. A trace becomes a validated signal.

## Visual grammar

- An open square marks an artifact, relationship, or unresolved hypothesis.
- Thin, straight connectors identify relationships. Connect to relevant excerpts, not empty containers. Use orthogonal connections without decorative arrowheads or diagonal dividers.
- Grayscale highlights select context without implying correctness.
- A filled teal square terminates a validated finding. Teal highlights its supporting evidence. Never use it for invented scores, arbitrary stages, access labels, or decorative activity.
- A solid enclosure expresses an environment or processing boundary. A capped neutral line expresses the end of source-code use. Both are separate meanings from a validated signal.
- Name each relationship and show the evidence that makes it relevant.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Use |

| Validated signal | #0E8E7E | Validated findings and supporting evidence |

| Structure | #C0C6CC | Thin connectors and separators |

## Typography and composition

IBM Plex Sans supplies headings and prose. JetBrains Mono supplies source references, code, relationship labels, and states. Headings use weight 500, deliberate line breaks, comfortable leading, and tight tracking. Prose uses weight 400.

Vary composition while preserving semantics. A hero condenses a trace. An evidence view exposes its sources. The operating model lets someone inspect stages. Integration identifies authorized inputs. Security encloses processing and explains the source-code lifecycle.

On mobile, preserve reading order and stack branches. Do not shrink a desktop diagram into illegible text. Essential evidence must remain available without hover or animation.

## Motion and interaction

Evidence is visible initially. A user-requested replay reveals change, context, hypothesis, validation, and finding once, in order. No continuous scanning or simulated live analysis. Honor reduced motion. Native disclosures reveal scope and stage details with keyboard and touch support.

## Truth and assets

Use the supplied wordmark and favicon assets. Label synthetic examples at their point of use. Do not present illustrative assertions as live runs, exact permission grants, actual repositories, or customer results. Do not invent supported providers, workflow destinations, certifications, or deployment mechanisms.

The website retains the existing two proof metrics and the user-requested Talk to team placeholder. Add the real calendar URL when supplied. Security statements reflect the owner's stated product commitments; the frontend is not independent verification of backend enforcement.

## Reuse beyond the website

A pitch slide connects one change to its context and consequence. A technical graphic uses the same open artifact and filled signal markers even when cropped to three excerpts. Never fabricate data to complete a composition. Keep these semantics consistent across the site, decks, and graphics.
