THE DETAILS

Clear before you connect.

Who needs a paid seat?

Only admin-approved developers whose pull requests Myco reviews need paid seats. People who only view findings are free.

What does the 30-day trial include?

Book a Myco introduction to get started. Your 30-day trial includes the full Team review pipeline, with unlimited reviews and repositories. No credit card is required. Bring your own LLM API key; your provider charges you for model usage during the trial.

What happens after 30 days?

Reviews pause until you subscribe. The trial does not automatically turn into a paid subscription.

How are LLM costs billed?

Your Myco subscription covers the review platform. You supply your own LLM API key and pay model usage directly to your provider. These costs are separate from Myco pricing, including during the trial.

Are there limits on reviews or repositories?

Both plans include unlimited reviews and repositories. Model usage is billed separately by your LLM provider and depends on your review activity.

How does annual billing work?

Team costs $16 per developer per month when billed annually, paid as $192 per developer per year. Monthly billing is $20 per developer per month.

What deployment options does Enterprise offer?

Enterprise offers single-tenant SaaS or on-prem deployment, alongside custom connectors, identity controls, audit logs and dedicated support. Talk to us about your requirements.
